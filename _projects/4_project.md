### Overview
Synthesizable 4-bit Pipelined Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) supporting arithmetic (addition, subtraction), logical (AND, OR, XOR), and shift operations. Implemented at the micro-architectural level with operation decoding and validated on FPGA hardware.
