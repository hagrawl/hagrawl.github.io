### Overview
RTL micro-architecture and block management for multiple ARM Non-Coherent Interconnects and digital IPs.

### Key Highlights
- **Protocols Supported:** AXI4, AXI5, CHI-E, APB, and UART.
- **RTL Wrappers:** Developed synthesizable wrappers for digital IPs with dedicated testbenches.
- **Verification & Lint:** Extensive static analysis (Lint) and functional validation using QuestaSim, VCS, and Visualizer.
