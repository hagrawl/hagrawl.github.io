I am a **Senior Lead Design Engineer** at **NXP Semiconductors** in Bangalore, focusing on **Non-Coherent Interconnect & Last Level Cache Design**. Starting **Fall 2026**, I will be joining the **University of Southampton** to pursue my **MSc in Microelectronics System Design**.

Previously, I spent over three years as an Engineer at **ARM Ltd.**, managing core blocks across multiple ARM Non-Coherent Interconnects and driving digital IP integration with communication protocols including **AXI4/5, CHI-E, and APB**. Earlier, I worked on software-programmable hardware acceleration at **AMD (Xilinx)** using Vitis HLS and OpenCL for Alveo accelerator cards. I graduated with a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (VLSI Major) from Lovely Professional University.

**Research & Exploration:** I am actively interested in open research topics across **FPGAs, Domain-Specific Hardware Accelerators, Parallel Micro-architectures, and GPU/Heterogeneous Systems**.
