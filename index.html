I am a Senior Lead Design Engineer at NXP Semiconductors in Bangalore, specializing in Non-Coherent Interconnect (NIC) & Last Level Cache (LLC) Design.

Previously, I spent over 3 years as an Engineer at ARM Ltd. working on Non-Coherent Interconnect micro-architecture, digital IP wrappers, and SoC integration across AXI4/5, CHI-E, APB, and UART protocols. Before ARM, I developed hardware-accelerated FPGA kernels at AMD (Xilinx) targeting Alveo devices (U200, U250) and Versal adaptive SoCs using Vitis HLS and OpenCL.

I completed my B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering with a major in VLSI from Lovely Professional University.
